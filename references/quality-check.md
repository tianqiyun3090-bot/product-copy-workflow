# 成稿质检

Run this check silently before delivering external-facing copy. Report only an issue that needs the user's decision or a material change you made.

## Required checks

1. **事实依据**：Every material product, credential, price, result, comparison, and availability claim is present in the evidence ledger and marked 可宣传.
2. **边界与风险**：Dynamic policy claims, data handling, important external content, and customer-result claims retain any necessary qualification or human-review boundary.
3. **夸大表达**：Remove absolute, guaranteed, exclusive, permanent, or unsupported superiority claims. Do not substitute a weaker but still unverified claim.
4. **客户语言**：The pain, outcome, and title are concrete enough for the selected customer group and do not rely on empty marketing labels.
5. **渠道适配**：Respect the requested count, length, title limit, structure, and call to action for the intended channel.
6. **可读性**：Remove duplicated hooks, repeated sentence structures, overly long sentences, and unnecessary jargon.
7. **行动引导**：The next step is clear, proportionate to the request, and supported by the actual product or service arrangement.

## High-stakes topics

When copy concerns medical, financial, legal, education or employment outcomes, revenue, investment, or another high-stakes claim, state that a qualified human review is still needed. Do not imply compliance clearance.
