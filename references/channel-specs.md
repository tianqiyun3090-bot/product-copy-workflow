# 渠道规格

Use these defaults only when the user has not provided a format, length, or count requirement. The user's explicit requirements take precedence.

## 朋友圈

- Start with one familiar work or life situation, then add a viewpoint and a single natural action.
- Prefer 120–250 Chinese characters for a short post; use line breaks for mobile readability.
- Avoid stacking every selling point into one post. Make a single point persuasive and concrete.

## 海报

- Main title: default to no more than 18 Chinese characters.
- Subtitle: default to no more than 30 Chinese characters.
- The title should carry one promise of value or a familiar pain; the subtitle explains the concrete method, audience, or scenario.
- Do not put claims that need qualification into a visual headline.

## 小红书

- Provide varied title directions before choosing a body angle where useful.
- Open with a work scene, question, misconception, or useful method; avoid clickbait unsupported by the body.
- Prefer practical steps, boundaries, and examples from supplied materials over generic inspiration.

## 私信与咨询

- Start by acknowledging the inquiry, then ask one concise question about role, task, or current obstacle.
- Explain only the course or product element relevant to the reply. Do not pressure a decision or imply an outcome.
- Use a low-friction next step, such as discussing fit, sharing the relevant arrangement, or booking a conversation when offered by the source.

## 视频口播

- Put the hook in the first three seconds, then move quickly to a familiar scene or a clear viewpoint.
- Use short sentences and natural transitions. A spoken script should not read like a long sales page.
- Treat timing as approximate. Adjust after an actual read-through rather than shrinking important facts to hit an exact second count.
